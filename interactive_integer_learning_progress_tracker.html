Understanding Integers

Integers are whole numbers that can be positive, negative, or zero (e.g., ..., -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, ...). Mastering how to add and subtract them is the foundation of algebra!

+

Rules for Addition

  • 1. Same Signs (Add & Keep) Add their absolute values and keep the common sign.
    (+4) + (+3) = +7
    (-4) + (-3) = -7
  • 2. Different Signs (Subtract & Keep Larger) Subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger one, and keep the sign of the number with the larger absolute value.
    (-8) + (+3) = -5 // 8 - 3 = 5, 8 is negative
    (+8) + (-3) = +5 // 8 - 3 = 5, 8 is positive
-

Rules for Subtraction

"Keep - Change - Change" (KCC)

Subtracting an integer is the exact same as adding its opposite! Follow these three steps:

  1. Keep the first number the same.
  2. Change the subtraction sign to addition.
  3. Change the sign of the second number to its opposite.
5 - (-3) → 5 + (+3) = 8
-4 - 6 → -4 + (-6) = -10
